Teams and Clubs

Every team and club uses the 96 box with its own name. Choose one to open it in the logo finder.

Need a logo for a group that isn't listed? Team logos follow fixed rules for long names, two-word names, and abbreviations (guidelines, page 8). New ones are added in source/kit.py.

Colors

Maroon and cream are the school colors. Black and white are for one-color printing. The gradient is for TV screens and digital backgrounds. Click a value to copy it.

Fonts

Everything uses the Saira family. It's free on Google Fonts and works in Google Docs, Slides, and Canva. The logos are artwork, so never type them out in a font.

Templates

Each template has an example and a blank background. The social templates also come as editable PowerPoint files: change the text, swap the photo, and export a PNG. They open in PowerPoint, Keynote, and Google Slides, and Canva can import them. Use the fonts above.
